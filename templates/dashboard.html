Growth Coordinator — New Realtor Outreach ▼

1. Standard New Realtor Outreach

"Hi, this is Rose with Golden Bear Inspection Group. Is this [Agent Name]? I know I'm catching you during your workday so I'll keep this brief. I work directly with Joe, the owner. We've been expanding relationships with local agents and your name came up. Joe really enjoys building long-term relationships with agents. I'd love to learn about your business and see if we could grab coffee sometime. (PAUSE) How long have you been in real estate? Are you mainly working buyers or sellers? What's something you wish inspectors did better?"

2. Voicemail Script (New RP)

"Hi [Agent Name], this is Rose with Golden Bear Inspection Group. I'm reaching out because Joe, our owner, has been connecting with local agents and we'd love to introduce ourselves. Feel free to give me a call back at [PHONE]. I'll also send a quick follow-up message. Have a great day!"

3. SMS Follow-Up (New RP)

"Hi [Agent Name], Rose with Golden Bear Inspection Group. Left a voicemail. Anything I can help with? Reply or call: [PHONE]."

Objection Handling & Special Situations ▼

8. If Annoyed / "Why are you calling?"

"I'll be quick — we're just reconnecting with local agents to keep relationships active and see if there's anything we can improve."

9. If They Ask "What's in it for us?"

"We focus on making inspections easier for agents and clients — clear reports, reliable scheduling, and good communication. Joe enjoys building long-term partnerships to support your transactions."

10. If They Say "Who are you? I don't remember you"

"I work with Joe at Golden Bear Inspection Group. We do residential inspections in [area]. We've worked with your office in the past — I just wanted to reconnect."

11. If They Ask About Pricing/Benefits

"I can get you pricing details — what type of properties do you mainly work with? I'll send it by email."

12. If They Give Feedback/Complaint

"Thank you for telling me; that's helpful. I'll note this and share it with Joe so we can improve."

Closing Line (Always)

"Thanks for your time — I'll log this and follow up by [email/text/phone] in a few weeks. Appreciate it."
